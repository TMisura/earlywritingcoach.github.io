Print at home

Monthly drawing and writing booklets

The original Early Writing Coach offer is a booklet you print at home: a little drawing, a little writing, no special cueing program required. When a page is done, submit that writing here like any other school assignment.

How to use a booklet with this coach

  1. Print the month’s booklet and keep the paper copy — that is the file that matters at home.
  2. The child draws and writes on the page. You do not need our prompt set; school work is fine too.
  3. When you want help, type or paste the writing into Submit writing.
  4. Save the coaching note (Downloads or print). We do not keep the page.

Printable PDF packets by month are the next production file to add. Until those are uploaded, use any notebook page or school assignment the same way.
